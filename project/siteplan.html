1. Site Name

PN

Providiahnelly

Step Out in Style

The name of my website is Providiahnelly. It represents my footwear business, which focuses on providing fashionable and comfortable footwear for different customers.

I selected this name because it gives my business a unique identity and helps establish a recognizable brand. It is also a name that can grow with the business as more footwear designs and collections are introduced.

My slogan, "Step Out in Style," communicates the brand's commitment to helping customers look good and feel comfortable wherever they go.

Proposed website name: Providiahnelly

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the Providiahnelly website is to promote my footwear business and provide customers with an easy way to discover, explore, and enquire about available footwear.

The website will showcase different footwear collections, including slippers, sandals, and palm slippers for men, women, and children. Each product will include photographs, descriptions, prices, and relevant details to help customers make informed choices.

Visitors will also be able to learn about the business, explore available collections, and contact the business through an online enquiry and order request form.

Target Audience

  • Men looking for comfortable and fashionable footwear.
  • Women interested in attractive slippers and sandals.
  • Parents searching for suitable footwear for their children.
  • People who want quality footwear at reasonable prices.
  • Customers who want to enquire about products or place orders online.

Planned Website Pages

Page Content and Purpose
Home Introduces Providiahnelly, displays the brand slogan, featured products, and footwear categories.
Collections Displays slippers, sandals, and palm slippers with product images, descriptions, and prices.
Contact Provides contact information and an enquiry form for customers who want to ask questions or place orders.
References Identifies the sources of photographs and external materials used on the website.

5. Typography

The website will use two font families: Montserrat and Open Sans. These fonts are modern, professional, and easy to read.

Montserrat

Providiahnelly – Step Out in Style

Montserrat will be used for the website logo, main headings, section headings, product names, and navigation headings. Its bold and modern appearance will give the brand a strong visual identity.

Open Sans

Discover stylish and comfortable footwear designed for everyday life.

Open Sans will be used for paragraphs, product descriptions, prices, form labels, and general website content. It provides good readability across different screen sizes.

7. Planned Website Features

The final Providiahnelly website will use HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to provide an interactive and responsive experience.

  • Responsive navigation for desktop and mobile devices.
  • Product collections for men, women, and children.
  • JavaScript arrays and objects to organize product information.
  • Dynamic product cards generated using JavaScript.
  • Product category filtering.
  • Customer contact and order enquiry form.
  • JavaScript form validation and event handling.
  • Template literals for dynamically generated HTML.
  • Local storage for saving selected products or customer preferences.
  • Optimized images and native lazy loading.
